Titles, authors, affiliations and session names come from the public ISSCC and VLSI Symposium advance programs; links go to the paper's DOI or an IEEE Xplore search. No abstracts or PDFs are hosted here.

Scope: sessions run by the digital architectures, digital circuits, machine-learning and security committees, plus digital papers picked out of mixed sessions by keyword rules. Topic tags are assigned from titles by keyword rules, so treat them as a first pass, not ground truth. Rules and overrides live in the repository.
